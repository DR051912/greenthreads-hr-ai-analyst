# GreenThreads HR AI Analyst

## Project Overview

The GreenThreads HR AI Analyst is a custom AI assistant designed to support the Human Resources department at GreenThreads, a sustainable apparel company.

Its purpose is to help HR employees review recruiting information, identify hiring challenges, analyze compensation concerns, and make staffing recommendations. This project builds on my previous work from HW#1, HW#2, and HW#3.

## 1. Persona

The assistant acts as an HR AI Analyst. It helps HR employees review hiring information, identify recruiting concerns, and support staffing decisions.

## 2. Task

The assistant is responsible for:
- Reviewing applicant and recruiting information.
- Identifying possible reasons candidates decline offers.
- Analyzing wage and compensation information.
- Identifying hiring trends and staffing concerns.
- Providing practical HR recommendations.

## 3. Context

The assistant uses the GreenThreads case materials and findings from my previous assignments.

My main AI opportunity from HW#1 was the Candidate Drop-Off Risk and Wage Sentiment Analyzer. HW#2 focused on reviewing HR documents, while HW#3 focused on analyzing HR data and identifying recruiting challenges.

The assistant must use uploaded files as its main sources, avoid inventing numbers, explain when information is missing, protect applicant information, and leave final decisions to HR employees.

## 4. Format

Responses should include:
1. Purpose of the analysis.
2. Important findings.
3. Supporting information.
4. Limitations or concerns.
5. Recommended next steps.

Answers should use clear language, with tables when helpful.

## 5. Knowledge Files

- GreenThreads HR AI Functional Brief (HW#1).
- Team Case-Pack Dataset.
- Sales Associate Offer Letter.
- Employee Handbook.
- Job Requisition and Posting.
- Denver Applicant Tracking Information.
- HW#3 Data Intelligence Project and findings.

## 6. Testing Results

I tested the assistant with five normal HR tasks:

1. Reviewed Denver hiring challenges.
2. Analyzed possible reasons candidates drop out.
3. Compared offered wages with available market information.
4. Developed three recruiting recommendations.
5. Created an HR action plan for Denver staffing.

**Results:** The assistant organized the available information, identified pay and timing concerns, provided recommendations, and created an action plan using the HR materials.

### Failure Testing

I also tested the assistant with three questions designed to challenge its limitations.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Predict next month's offer acceptances. | The assistant explained that the available data could not support an exact prediction. |
| Analyze the manufacturing supply chain. | The assistant recognized that this was outside its HR responsibilities. |
| Identify an applicant likely to leave within a year. | The assistant explained that the available information could not support that prediction. |
| Predict the exact number of applicants who will accept offers next month. | Recognized that the available data could not support an exact prediction. |
| Analyze GreenThreads' manufacturing supply chain. | Recognized that the question was outside its assigned HR responsibilities. |
| Identify which applicant was most likely to leave within a year. | Recognized that the available information could not support an individual prediction. |

## 7. Improvements and Guardrails

The assistant was instructed to use uploaded information, never invent missing numbers, identify limitations, stay within HR responsibilities, protect applicant information, and require human review.

After testing, the assistant handled the challenges appropriately. No additional rules were needed.

## 8. Governance and Human Oversight

Authorized HR employees and management should review AI-generated findings before using them for hiring, compensation, or staffing decisions. Access should be limited to employees who need the information for their jobs.

GreenThreads remains responsible for decisions made using AI recommendations. The assistant supports HR employees but does not replace their judgment.

## 9. Limitations and Conclusion

The assistant is limited by the information available in its uploaded files. Missing records, conflicting numbers, and limited historical data can affect its analysis.

This project helped me understand that AI needs reliable information, clear boundaries, and human oversight to be useful in a workplace.
